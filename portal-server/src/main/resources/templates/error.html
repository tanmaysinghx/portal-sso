Error 400 (Bad Request)

Authorization Request Rejected

Portal SSO could not process the authentication request.

OAuth 2.0 Parameter: redirect_uri
Likely Causes & Fixes:
  • Redirect URI Mismatch: In OAuth 2.1, the redirect_uri sent by your app must exactly match one of the URIs registered in the Portal SSO Admin Console under Applications → OAuth Clients (including scheme https://, port, and exact path).
  • Unknown or Disabled Client ID: Ensure the client_id parameter matches an enabled client in Portal SSO.
  • Unregistered Scopes: Verify that all requested scopes (e.g. openid profile email) are granted to this client.
