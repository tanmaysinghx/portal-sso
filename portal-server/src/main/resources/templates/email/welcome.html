One sign-in gets you into every application your organisation has connected to Portal SSO. Sign in once and the apps you have access to are listed on your home screen.

Turn on two-factor authentication

Your account unlocks every connected application, which makes it worth a second factor. It takes about a minute under My Account.

Missing an application?

Some apps are restricted. If you cannot see one you need, request access from your home screen and an administrator will review it.
